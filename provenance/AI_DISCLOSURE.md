# Provenance and Disclosure Contract

## Public truthfulness

The film must never be presented as archival evidence of an existing municipality, citizen vote, public project or deployed PNyX institution.

Required public disclosure:

> **Η ταινία είναι έργο speculative documentary. Οι χαρακτήρες και τα γεγονότα είναι μυθοπλαστικές συνθέσεις. Απεικονίζει μία πιθανή δημόσια πραγματικότητα και όχι υπαρκτή διοικητική διαδικασία ή καταγεγραμμένο γεγονός.**

A shorter platform description may use:

> **A fictional documentary from a civic reality that could exist.**

## Production record per asset

Record:

- asset ID;
- scene and shot;
- creation date;
- human author/approver;
- model and model version;
- tool/platform;
- complete prompt;
- reference asset IDs;
- generation ID and seed when available;
- source licenses;
- transformations and edit history;
- synthetic voice identity and consent basis;
- factual claims added in post;
- approval status;
- rejection reason for discarded takes.

## Character integrity

- All principal people are fictional composites.
- Do not imitate a real private person without explicit consent.
- Do not clone a real voice without explicit consent and a recorded license.
- Child characters use invented identity and appropriately licensed performer/reference material.
- Never generate a real public official making statements they did not make.

## Civic-data integrity

- Costs, delays, vote totals and audit findings in this pilot are fictional production data.
- Label them within the film's fictional case file, not as claims about current Greek authorities.
- Real standards or laws, when later introduced, require a cited research dossier and legal review.

## Repository alignment

The documentary may borrow PNyX's public conceptual canon. Production-specific characters, dialogue, shot prompts and generated assets remain separately versioned so that civic specification and cinematic fiction never become indistinguishable.

## Release checklist

- Disclosure appears in description and end credits.
- No generated text is mistaken for an authentic public document.
- Every real third-party asset has a recorded license.
- Every synthetic voice has a recorded authorization basis.
- The film distinguishes present fact, PNyX proposal and fictional future depiction.
- The public production manifest is exported with the release.
