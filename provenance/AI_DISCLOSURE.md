# Provenance and Disclosure Contract

## Public truthfulness

The film must never be presented as archival evidence of an existing municipality, citizen vote, public project, or deployed PNyX institution.

Required public disclosure:

> **This film is a work of speculative documentary. Its characters and events are fictional composites. It depicts a possible public reality, not an existing administrative process or a documented event.**

The final on-film disclosure **MUST use this English text exactly**. Do not substitute the earlier Greek version.

A shorter platform description may use:

> **A fictional documentary from a civic reality that could exist.**

## Production record per asset

Record the asset ID, scene and shot, creation date, human author/approver, model and version, tool/platform, complete prompt, references, generation ID and seed when available, source licenses, transformations, edit history, synthetic voice authorization, factual claims added in post, approval status, and rejection reason for discarded takes.

## Character integrity

- All principal people are fictional composites.
- Do not imitate a real private person or clone a real voice without explicit consent and a recorded license.
- Child characters use invented identity and appropriately licensed performer/reference material.
- Never generate a real public official making statements they did not make.

## Civic-data integrity

- Costs, delays, vote totals, and audit findings in this pilot are fictional production data.
- Label them within the film's fictional case file, not as claims about current Greek authorities.
- Real standards or laws require a cited research dossier and legal review.

## Repository alignment

Production-specific characters, dialogue, shot prompts, and generated assets remain separately versioned so that civic specification and cinematic fiction never become indistinguishable.

## Release checklist

- The exact English disclosure appears in the description and end credits.
- No generated text is mistaken for an authentic public document.
- Every real third-party asset has a recorded license.
- Every synthetic voice has a recorded authorization basis.
- The film distinguishes present fact, PNyX proposal, and fictional future depiction.
- The public production manifest is exported with the release.
