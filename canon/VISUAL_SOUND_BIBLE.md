# Visual and Sound Bible

## Camera

- Native 24fps, 180-degree shutter, 1/48 exposure.
- Vintage spherical or restrained anamorphic character; no extreme flares.
- Lenses: 28mm for lived environment, 40–50mm for dialogue, 85mm only for isolated observation.
- Handheld operator body weight, calm enough to observe.
- Dynamic register mostly Tier 1–2; construction may reach Tier 3 briefly.
- Eye level belongs to the subject, including wheelchair height.
- No drone establishing shot unless the geography itself becomes evidence.

## Image texture

- Fine 35mm grain, restrained halation, real skin texture.
- Open shadow detail, rolled highlights, bright Greek daylight allowed to feel hard.
- Dust and haze create depth; do not turn the world permanently golden.
- Editorial documentary grade, never product-commercial polish.

## Locations

### The playground before

Aging municipal playground between apartment blocks. Sun-faded rubber tiles lifting at joins, narrow metal gate, one worn swing frame, patchy shade, low concrete wall used as informal seating, compacted dirt beyond the paved route, ordinary graffiti and repairs. No picturesque ruin.

### Eleni's kitchen

Small rented apartment kitchen, practical rather than poor: refrigerator magnets, school schedule, mismatched mugs, weak dawn light, fluorescent strip, laundry visible through a half-open door.

### Evidence room

Municipal multipurpose room, stackable chairs, two folding tables, neutral walls, fluorescent ceiling light mixed with window daylight, laptops, printed maps, rulers, survey photographs, water bottles. No glass command center.

### Public deliberation courtyard

Municipal courtyard or schoolyard at dusk, mismatched chairs, one simple microphone, extension cables, paper briefings, wall with old paint, nearby traffic audible. Fifty people maximum; most affected citizens remain off-screen or absent.

### Construction

Real small municipal worksite: mesh fencing, dust, material pallets, rainwater, narrow access, workers in used PPE, adjacent residents continuing ordinary life.

### Playground after

Clearly improved but not pristine: accessible continuous route, mixed play equipment, seating preserved, new drainage, one unfinished planting bed, slight dust at perimeter, ordinary use.

## Interface and documents

- Never ask video models to generate exact Greek text.
- Screens display abstract neutral blocks or are framed obliquely.
- Exact PNyX UI, charts, case number, vote and audit data are composited in post.
- UI is quiet, legible, public-administration grade; white/charcoal, restrained accent, no neon gradients.
- Every displayed claim can be traced to an evidence object or a recorded human statement.

## Sound

- Diegetic sound leads: buses, kettles, wheel rims, metal gate, paper, fluorescent hum, chairs, distant traffic, rain on fencing, construction tools, children outside frame.
- Dialogue is close but not studio-clean; room tone remains.
- No inspirational piano under deliberation.
- Optional score appears only in the final reuse sequence: sparse acoustic and electronic texture built from field-recorded mechanical and human sounds.
- Silence after difficult statements is preserved.

## Editing rhythm

- Early life scenes hold longer than platform/process scenes.
- Analysis becomes legible through repeated physical actions, not rapid techno montage.
- The decision is brief.
- Execution receives as much screen time as deliberation.
- The audit is not an epilogue footnote; it is the completion of the case.
