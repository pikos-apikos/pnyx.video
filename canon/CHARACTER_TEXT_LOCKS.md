# Character Locks

All characters are fictional composites. Generate canonical face references before any scene work. Preserve age, fatigue, ordinary wardrobe and physical behavior. Avoid model-perfect symmetry and commercial styling.

## 1 — Ελένη

**Face/identity lock:** Greek woman, 39, medium olive skin with natural unevenness and warm undertone, compact oval face, defined but not sharp jaw, dark brown eyes with mild night-shift fatigue, straight medium nose, medium lips, dark brown shoulder-length hair with a few early gray strands, usually loosely tied, small silver stud earrings.

**Default wardrobe:** washed navy cotton scrub trousers under a plain beige cardigan after work; on civic days, faded blue jeans, cream cotton shirt, canvas shoulder bag, practical flat shoes.

**Never:** polished presenter makeup, power suit, aspirational-tech-founder styling, permanent smile.

## 2 — Μυρτώ

**Face/identity lock:** Greek girl, 12, warm fair-olive skin, round-oval face, dark expressive eyes, thick dark eyebrows, dense dark curls tied high with loose temple curls, direct evaluating expression.

**Mobility lock:** matte dark-blue manual wheelchair with ordinary scuffs, black push rims, compact frame fitted to her body, colorful fabric backpack attached behind. Chair dimensions and color never drift.

**Default wardrobe:** muted red cotton T-shirt, olive shorts or dark leggings depending scene, worn white trainers.

**Never:** angelic lighting, pity framing, exaggerated bravery, pristine medical-device aesthetic.

## 3 — Θανάσης

**Face/identity lock:** Greek man, 71, weathered olive skin, rectangular face, short white hair, gray moustache, deep-set brown eyes, sun marks, broad nose, heavy retired-tradesman hands, reading glasses in shirt pocket.

**Default wardrobe:** faded blue-and-cream checked short-sleeve shirt, charcoal trousers, old brown leather belt, dark work shoes.

**Never:** villainous scowl, caricatured angry pensioner, luxury clothing, party-political symbols.

## 4 — Άννα

**Face/identity lock:** Greek woman, 45, light olive skin, lean angular face, hazel eyes, straight dark-blonde chin-length bob tucked behind left ear, faint forehead lines, minimal makeup.

**Default wardrobe:** pale blue or off-white cotton shirt with sleeves rolled, dark work trousers, scuffed black safety shoes; orange survey vest and white helmet only where physically required.

**Never:** sterile corporate blazer, glossy architect editorial look, omniscient confidence.

## Group behavior lock

- Nobody looks continuously toward camera.
- People interrupt, search papers, misunderstand and pause.
- Eye contact follows the actual speaker.
- Hands perform concrete tasks.
- Background participants vary in age, body, dress and attention.
- No synchronized nodding, unanimous smiling or staged diversity lineup.
