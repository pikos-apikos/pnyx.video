# AAIFF 2026 — production target

Verified against the official Astana AI Film Festival website on 7 August 2026.

## Submission deadline

The current official deadline is **15 August 2026**.

## Requirements relevant to this production

- Minimum film duration: **3 minutes**.
- Generative AI must be integral to the work.
- Contributors and the AI production pipeline must be disclosed.
- Films may be in any language, but English subtitles must be embedded in the video.
- Submission is by public/unlisted YouTube or a viewable Google Drive link.
- YouTube submissions require `#SpecialForAAIFF` in the description.
- The 2026 thematic competition is built around **“The Future Worth Living In”**.
- A work may be registered separately for both the thematic and open competitions.

## Implication for «ΠΝΥΞ: Η Παιδική Χαρά»

The contest does not redefine the story. The film already explores a possible civic reality through ordinary human behaviour. It does, however, create a fixed production clock.

Before generating more downstream assets, the production should explicitly lock whether it is finishing the original 11–13 minute treatment or a tighter festival cut derived from the same canon.
