## Approved copy

### 1

**1 — THE PROBLEM IS NOT YET A PROPOSAL**

Before deciding what to build, the neighborhood must define the problem: who is affected, what is known, and what would count as solved.

### 2

**2 — THE ROOM OF CONSEQUENCES**

Every proposal has consequences. Before deciding, PNyx asks who benefits, who pays, what is known, and what remains uncertain.

### 3

**3 — THE PERSON WHO LOSES**

The analysis reveals another affected group: residents who depend on the playground as their only open meeting place. Thanasis gives that loss a voice.

### 4

**4 — THE PUBLIC BRIEFING**

Public information must be usable, not merely available. Residents receive a short summary, a detailed brief, and access to the complete evidence.

### 5

**5 — THE PUBLIC CONSULTATION**

Absence is not treated as consent. Facts, estimates, proposals and uncertainties are separated before the original plan is publicly revised.

### 6

**6 — THE DECISION**

The revised proposal is approved, but disagreement is not erased. Thanasis votes against it. In the public record, his vote remains anonymous, while his argument is preserved without exposing any personal information. A single proof of residence is enough to participate.

### 7

**7 — THE MANDATE MEETS REALITY**

The mandate is not a blank cheque. Delays, unexpected costs and requested changes remain public, reviewable and bound by the original decision.

### 8

**8 — THE PLAYGROUND OPENS**

The playground opens without ceremony. Myrto enters, Thanasis remains unconvinced, and the first visible defect is recorded rather than hidden.

### 9

**9 — THE PUBLIC ACCOUNT**

Six months later, public memory compares promise and outcome: delays, added costs, corrected failures, open complaints, and knowledge others can reuse.

### Epilogue

**EPILOGUE — THE NEXT NEIGHBORHOOD**

Another neighborhood begins with the full public record, inheriting the specifications, disagreements, mistakes and lessons of the first loop.

1. Eleni states the unresolved problem.
2. The clarification asks who is affected.
3. Myrto makes the direct access failure visible.
4. The consequences analysis reveals the less obvious loss of shared open space.
5. Thanasis gives that secondary loss a voice.

Do not present PNyx as magically discovering either person. The edit must show the causal path from clarification to direct impact, then from consequences analysis to indirect loss.
